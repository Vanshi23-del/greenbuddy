# 🌱 GreenBuddy — Your AI-Powered Nature Companion

**Small actions. Greener choices. A better planet. 🌎**

GreenBuddy is an AI-powered environmental web application built to help people develop greener habits and spend more time away from screens. It combines practical environmental guidance with simple outdoor activities to encourage people to reconnect with nature.

Built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Node.js, Express, and Google's open-weight Gemma model running locally through Ollama.

## 🌿 Features

### 🌳 1. Outdoor Activity Planner

* Suggests activities based on your mood and available time.
* Encourages walking, observing birds, exploring plants, and enjoying nature.
* Includes a mission and nature-friendly reminders.
* Lets users mark an outdoor activity as completed.

### 🤖 2. Local Gemma AI Assistant

* Answers environmental questions in simple language.
* Provides tips about recycling, reducing plastic, and saving water and electricity.
* Uses the Gemma 3 1B model through Ollama on the local machine.

### ♻️ 3. AI Waste Sorting Helper

* Helps users understand how everyday waste may be sorted or disposed of.
* Offers general guidance about recycling, reuse, composting, and waste reduction.
* Reminds users that local waste disposal rules may vary.

### 🌱 4. Touch Grass Daily Challenge

* Encourages users to spend time outdoors.
* Includes three small nature-related tasks.
* Tracks daily progress with a progress bar.
* Saves completed tasks in browser local storage.
